04 · Go Deeper into the Psalm
Four Facts About Psalm 119:105
Understanding the Scripture

There is so much to learn about every word and verse in the Bible. Here are a few facts about this Psalm.

Fact 01 of 04
Psalm 119 is the longest chapter in the Bible at 176 verses.
Fact 02 of 04
It is written as an acrostic poem in Hebrew.
An acrostic poem is a poem where the first letter of each line or section spells out a word, phrase, or follows a sequence, in this case the Hebrew alphabet.
Fact 03 of 04
It is divided into 22 sections, one for each letter of the Hebrew alphabet.
Each section contains 8 verses that all begin with the same Hebrew letter.
Fact 04 of 04
Verse 105 opens the Nun section, the 14th letter of the Hebrew alphabet.

How This Was Made
The Custom 3D Viewer Process ↓

I wanted people to experience more than just video renders or screenshots of my 3D illustrations. I wanted them to be able to step inside the creative piece and explore it themselves.

The challenge was finding a way to present my GLB files in real time while staying true to my creative vision. Standard real-time viewers didn't give me the control I needed over lighting, atmosphere, and post-processing. So I built a custom viewer and editor using Babylon.js, a WebGL rendering engine, that allowed me to fine-tune every aspect of the illustration: lighting, glow, bloom, depth of field, chromatic aberration, halftone effects, and more. Once I had the look I wanted, I could share it as a single link that opens with all of those settings already applied.

This is a growing work in progress, but I am encouraged by what is possible at this early stage.
